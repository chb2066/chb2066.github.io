Project Experiences

Multi-Label Classification of Korean Traditional Patterns

Jun. 2026 – Aug. 2026
Hanbat National University AiRLab (ETRI-funded project)
  • Mapped traditional pattern images to 22 aesthetic adjective labels under severe class imbalance, with auxiliary text metadata permitted as additional input
  • Initially framed the task as image-text alignment and fine-tuned a state-of-the-art VLM (FG-CLIP2) end-to-end, reaching F1@5 0.62; image-only backbones (ConvNeXt and others) fine-tuned under the same protocol failed to improve on this, while a text-only baseline reached 0.75–0.77
  • Reproduction of the same failure across architecturally distinct vision models isolated weak visual representation, not the alignment objective, as the bottleneck, redirecting the design from joint alignment to late fusion of independently extracted embeddings
  • Built a late-fusion pipeline: frozen DINOv3 and fine-tuned BERT-family text embeddings projected into 2 tokens, concatenated, and processed by self-attention before a linear classification head; ensembled across text encoders (F1@5 0.80)

Abnormal Behavior and Anomaly Detection in Subway Stations

May 2026 – Jul. 2026
Hanbat National University AiRLab
  • Constructed the training set from AI-Hub subway station footage combined with self-collected video across varied indoor environments (lab, auditorium, research facility), as no public dataset covered the target behaviors
  • Trained two separately fine-tuned transformer-based detectors rather than a single joint model, as the two target types differ in appearance and temporal characteristics: hazards (fire/smoke, 0.90 mAP@50) and abnormal behavior (fall/crouch, 0.95 mAP@50)
  • Suppressed false positives where static light glare was detected as smoke, by using bounding-box coordinate variation between adjacent frames to filter out stationary sources

Satellite Image Building Area Segmentation

Jan. 2026 – Feb. 2026
Self-directed study on the 2023 SW-Centered University AI Competition dataset (Dacon)
  • Traced consistently low inference performance, reproduced identically across three architecturally distinct models, to resolution and image-quality degradation between the train and test data, isolating it as a data-handling issue rather than model capacity
  • Replaced resize-based resolution matching with crop-based training and padding-based inference, then trained at the 224 test resolution to recover small buildings (IoU 0.79)
  • Targeted residual failure modes identified through prediction visualization with shadow augmentation and random resized crop, combined via weighted ensembling
