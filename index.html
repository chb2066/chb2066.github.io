About

I am an undergraduate researcher interested in how machines perceive and reason about the world through multiple modalities. I focus on reading papers at their architectural depth, implementing them from scratch, and documenting my findings to bridge the gap between theory and practice.

My current research interests center around vision-language models (VLM/VLA), multimodal learning, object detection, and knowledge distillation.

Selected Projects

Maritime Multimodal QA LLM

Multimodal question answering over maritime imagery, combining a vision encoder with a language model for scene-grounded responses.

Persona LLM for Painting-Style Feedback

A RAG pipeline that evaluates traditional paintings with the Six Canons (六法) framework, combining CLIP, FAISS, and a vision-language model.

Satellite Building Segmentation

Building extraction from satellite imagery, benchmarking segmentation models and refining boundary precision with post-processing.

AI-Generated Text Detection

Competition project: document-level binary detection (decoder-only + LoRA) and sentence-level AI-ratio prediction (encoder-only, per-sentence prediction with aggregation).
